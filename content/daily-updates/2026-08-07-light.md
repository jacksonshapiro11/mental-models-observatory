## Friday, August 7, 2026

### Nine Hundred Million Shares and No Seller

*Nine hundred and eleven million SpaceX shares became legal to sell on Thursday and the stock rose, which is the day's exception and the reason to notice the rule. Four other stories broke the other way on the same hinge: a retaliation package aimed at auditors, a costless ban held in place by $762 million somebody else had already sunk, the week's largest round priced days after two agencies let a machine work alone, and a chokepoint talk that swapped its deadline for a condition. The binding constraint was never the object. It was permission to use it, and the price moved only where permission was withheld.*

## ▸ THE UPDATE

**SpaceX printed its worst close ever the day BEFORE 911.5 million shares became legal to sell, then rose on the day itself.**
It sank almost 14 percent to $108.27 Wednesday, then rallied Thursday as the first lockup took the public float from 4.9 percent to 11.8. Facebook released 773 million shares in November 2012 and rose 12.6 percent, while its far smaller August expiry had left it at its worst close to that point. Supply does not set the price. Whether the holder is a seller does. The cleared float is not the one that matters, though: only the first 20 percent could be sold on day one, and Musk stays locked until mid-2027.

**China retaliated this week by sanctioning the paperwork instead of the goods, because a tariff takes a percentage of an invoice and a missing certificate takes the whole shipment.**
Two of the entities Beijing listed make nothing. Altana Technologies clears Chinese exports through US customs, and the Responsible Business Alliance runs the forced-labour audits multinationals require of suppliers. Sanction a factory and you raise a price. Sanction the auditor and the goods have no legal path in at any price. It cuts both ways, though: an inspection Beijing refuses to host is also a cost its exporters stop carrying, so this binds only if the importer needs the paper more than the factory needs the sale.

**The market prices a September rate rise at a coin flip and nobody counts the votes, and a hike needs seven of twelve.**
Kalshi puts a September increase at 50 percent against 49 for no change. Twelve people vote, a majority is seven, and the Chair has no casting vote, so a six-six tie fails. On July 29 the committee held 9-3, with three regional presidents dissenting for a quarter point up. That hawkish base is three of twelve, so reaching seven means Kevin Warsh turning four colleagues who just voted the other way in public. Matt Klein's case does not need the roll call, though: if technology lifts the return on investment, rates rise because demand for capital rises, whoever votes.

**American concrete is buying a growing share of its key ingredient out of ash ponds rather than from operating power plants, and nobody has counted how much is down there.**
Concrete has been designed around fly ash, a coal-burning by-product, for fifty years, and nobody has ever made it on purpose. The 2024 industry survey holds both halves: production fell to 63.6 million tons from 66.7 while fly ash consumed in concrete rose to 14.6 million from 11.9. The extra is ash already ponded beside the stations, and a stock has a bottom in a way a flow does not. Ash that does not arrive is replaced by Portland cement. The association itself reads it the other way, though: ACAA says delayed plant closures plus expanding harvest mean ample supply for years, and its first study to actually size the harvested tonnage is still pending.

## ▸ MARKETS MINUTE

Nine of eleven sectors closed higher and the index still fell, and a benchmark that breaks in both directions inside forty-eight hours has stopped summarising the market. Rates moved as one, the 2s10s spread parked near 43 basis points, which reprices the policy anchor rather than growth. Gold rising a fourth straight session into higher yields is not a duration bid or an inflation fear, it is a question about who sets the number. Ether up 2.1 percent while bitcoin held one band a third session is a protocol bid, and those do not survive macro shocks.

## ▸ THE TAKE

A policy with no budget becomes permanent not by binding its author but by landing on top of money that was already sunk and cannot be refunded. On July 28 the FCC added foreign-produced advanced robotics to its Covered List, spending nothing, eighteen months after Ohio had already committed a $310 million grant plus a $452.2 million credit running to 2055 for Anduril's Arsenal-1. The agency did not summon that capital. It inherited it, and cannot now dismiss it. So through December 31, 2027 the determination moves one way: additions and tightenings, no narrowing that restores authorisation. It breaks on the seam Wisconsin found, promising Foxconn up to $2.85 billion in 2017 and renegotiating to $80 million in 2021, because performance credits are never sunk and half of Ohio's package is one. Judge industrial policy by the unrecoverable share of capital staked against it.

## ▸ INTERESTING THINGS

**A 1999 earthquake is still travelling up Taiwan's rivers, and everything ahead of it reads perfectly normal.**
The Chi-Chi quake lifted riverbeds along a thrust fault, and a river whose outlet level jumps does not re-grade evenly. It forms a step, and the step migrates upstream while every reach beyond it stays in exact equilibrium with a world that ended. Su-Chin Chen's team tracked twenty years of it across three Taiwanese rivers. Normal readings ahead of a front are not evidence of safety. They are evidence of distance.

**A switched-off nuclear reactor still glows, and somebody finally counted the light.** Double Chooz measured 106 antineutrino events at 5.9 sigma across 17.2 days of reactor-off data from 400 metres away, all of it from spent fuel and cooling pools. Inspectors can now audit a shut reactor from outside the fence.

**Wild capuchins choose a nut-cracking stone by whether it looks like somebody else already got it to work.** Given unused stones, 28 monkeys in Brazil sorted correctly by physics; add battering marks from prior use and that evidence overrode actual hardness. They learn from the discarded tool, never the toolmaker.

## ▸ THE MEDITATION

*"Leisure is only possible when a man is at one with himself."*
– Josef Pieper

Pieper wrote that in 1948, in a country that had spent fifteen years proving what happens when a society decides a person is what they produce. The direction of causation is the claim: you do not rest your way into peace with yourself. You have to be at some minimum peace before rest is available, and until then what looks like rest is the same restlessness in a different posture.

Which explains what you have filed as a personal failing. The vacation where you could not stop checking. The Sunday that somehow required reorganising a cupboard. The medieval word is acedia, and translating it as sloth is the mistake. Acedia is not the failure to move, it is the inability to stop, the restlessness of somebody too busy to hear a question about their life. The diagnostic is what happens in the first ten quiet minutes.

**Today's practice: build the ten minutes rather than resolve to take them. Phone in another room, timer set so you are not deciding when it ends. Each time the urge to do something arrives, write it down and stay seated. The list is the errands your mind offers when the alternative is a question.**

## ▸ THE MODEL

### Identity-Based Habits & Self-Concept

Every volunteer on Britain's Royal National Lifeboat Institution carries a pager to dinner, to weddings, to bed. It exists so that when it goes off there is no decision. The weighing happened once, years ago, and the pager is the receipt.

An identity is not what repetition produces. It is a compression algorithm for future decisions, replacing an expensive judgement you get wrong when tired with one cheap membership test. The cost lands where the evidence changes, because "should I keep doing this?" starts reading as "am I still who I said I was?" So run the Exit Test first: name the cost or date that would make this identity wrong for you, then ask what else you are holding with it. The right time to know how you would leave is while you have no reason to.
