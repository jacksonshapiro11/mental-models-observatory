**A data-retention clause, not price and not capability, is holding Anthropic's newest frontier model near 11 percent of what companies spend on Anthropic's own tools.**
The share comes from Ramp's card data across roughly 70,000 companies. The denominator matters: it is spending on that one vendor's tools, not on AI overall, which grew 38 percent in the same window. Roon, at OpenAI, wagered against his own employer's interest that 90 percent of the gap is the zero-data-retention policy, and JP Morgan corroborates him by restricting the model to new use cases only. The fact cutting the other way is in the same account. JP Morgan's reported complaint was that prices did not fall at launch, and a policy is convenient to blame when a launch does not undercut an incumbent. Anthropic has since begun offering enterprises use without the thirty-day custody window, which is what conceding looks like.

**The Dutch are cementing gas wells shut to stop a future government reopening them, and they are not doing it for safety or for the climate.**
About 500 billion cubic metres remain at Groningen, roughly twice what the European Union has imported from Russia since 2022. The 70 wells destroyed so far were inactive anyway, so the demolition changes none of the earthquake risk that closed the field. The government's own chief scientific advisor said mothballing was no worse environmentally and kept the option open. Pieter Garicano reads it as low confidence confessed: a decision made irreversible is one that would not survive an election. The case for the cement is that an unexercised option still costs money and keeps a region hostage to an argument the Dutch have lived for a decade. Michigan's Palisades plant, preserved rather than dismantled in 2022, is now restarting.

**The tariff refund was won in February and is being handed out by a clerk's calendar, so a large share of roughly $35 billion will expire rather than be denied.**
The Supreme Court held on 20 February that the IEEPA tariffs were collected without authority, but collecting is a separate proceeding. Customs opened a channel on 20 April for entries not yet final and did not reach entries already closed for good until late July. By 11 May it had cleared 8.3 million entries worth roughly $35.46 billion. An entry becomes final if nobody protests within 180 days of liquidation, and that clock ran through the months when nobody knew there would be anything to claim. The strongest argument against forfeiture is that a court forgives the deadline wholesale. The Court of International Trade has already ordered reliquidation without regard to those duties, and the government is appealing that question to the Federal Circuit. A right won at the top of a system still does not descend on its own.

## ▸ THE TAKE

The US Army this week put up to $2.2 billion behind small nuclear reactors on five of its own bases, the first due to run by September 2028. Under the Atomic Energy Act the Defense Department may build and operate reactors without a licence from the Nuclear Regulatory Commission, so the program is an order book and a bypass at once. A lead customer hands an expensive technology two gifts and only one is resellable: volume, which buys down unit cost and travels to every later buyer, and permission, which belongs to whoever granted it. We expect a reactor running on an Army base near the 2028 target and still no commercially licensed microreactor at a non-federal American site when it does. The serious objection is Rickover: the Navy also operated outside civilian licensing, and while permission never transferred, the designs did, the supply chain did, and the trained operators did. So when a government program gets called validation, ask which of the two gifts it is handing over.

## ▸ INTERESTING THINGS

**A stream rises minutes after the rain, and almost none of what comes out of it is the rain.**
Trace the isotopes and most of what leaves fell weeks or months earlier. Two speeds had been confused for one: celerity is how fast a pressure change travels through saturated ground, velocity is how fast the water itself travels, and they differ by orders of magnitude. Rain raises the head at the top of the hill and pushes out whatever was already sitting at the bottom. So when a change you made produces a fast result, look for a marker the new input carries and the old stock does not; if nobody can name one, you moved stored inventory rather than made anything.

**Animals have been quietly eating nature's own plastic for a very long time.** The enzymes that digest the biodegradable plastic bacteria store as an energy reserve turn out to be widespread across more than 66 animal species and 9 phyla, so a carbon pool everyone modelled as sealed inside bacteria has been feeding animal food webs all along.

**The largest galaxies of the early universe hide most of their mass in stars too faint to see.** Leiden astronomers found nine of them hold far more small dim stars than the supposedly universal birth ratio assumes, making one possibly four times more massive than estimated. An instrument does not merely fail to record what it cannot see; it rewrites the totals.

**The Grand Canyon's missing billion years may have been planed away by a cliff that walked across the continent before the Colorado River existed.** The breakup of Rodinia 800 million years ago raised a kilometre-high escarpment that retreated inland stripping up to eight kilometres of rock, so the visible dramatic cause was the last and smallest step.

## ▸ THE MEDITATION

Notice what this does not say. It is not about becoming less anxious. It is the anxiety changing jobs.

*"My real anxiety these days comes not from 'I don't got it' but from 'I could blow it.' It stems from the opportunity, from the stakes of the games I'm trying to play."*

– Zvi Mowshowitz

Early on, the fear is about capacity. Can I do this. Am I the sort of person who does. Everything we are taught about fear is built for that version: prepare more, practise more, get so good the doubt has nowhere to stand. It works, which is the problem, because it works so well that nobody notices when the fear stops being about capacity.

The second version arrives wearing the first one's clothes. You are afraid before the meeting, before the send, and you assume, as you always have, that the fear means you are not ready. So you prepare again. Its real content was: this one matters, and I get one pass.

The tell is the extra hour. Spend it drilling something you have done a hundred times and the diagnosis was capacity while the fear was stakes.

**Today's practice: take the thing you are most anxious about this week and ask, out loud. Am I afraid I cannot do this, or afraid of what it costs me if it goes badly? If it is the second, stop preparing, write down in one sentence what you would lose, and decide whether you accept that price.**

## ▸ THE MODEL

### Emergence & Complex Adaptive Systems

In 2018 a team led by Bert Blocken put 121 cyclists in a wind tunnel and measured what the formation actually saves. A rider buried in the middle of the pack meets as little as five to ten percent of the air resistance an isolated rider meets. No cyclist has that property: take one out, put them on the same road at the same speed, and they get none of it. The saving is not shared among the riders like a resource. It exists in the arrangement, and it vanishes the instant the arrangement does. You cannot buy the 90 percent. You can only buy riders.

**Before you try to buy, fix or assign a property, run the deletion test: could any single part have it alone, outside the group? If not, there are three levers, the rule each part follows, how densely they are packed, and the delay between one acting and another responding.**
