### Schools and Hospitals Outhired the Whole Economy

*Private employers added 38,000 jobs in August, and schools and hospitals added 45,000 of them, so every other sector put together shed workers. Hours later the machines that do some of that work reported revenue growth of 86 percent. The through-line is distribution: capacity to produce is compounding, the payroll meant to absorb it is not, and most of today's stories argue about who holds the difference. Tehran is the counter-instance and fits none of it.*

## ▸ THE UPDATE

**Every net job America added in August went to a school or a hospital, and a rate hike is still priced into it.**
Private payrolls grew 38,000, the smallest gain since January. Education and health services added 45,000 on their own, so every other sector combined shed workers. A hike is priced anyway: CME FedWatch has 66 percent odds on a quarter-point move on 16 September, while Kalshi and Polymarket sit at 48 and 49. Robin Brooks, the economist, calls a hike cheap insurance for anchoring that already worked, which would make the composition beside the point. The rival camp is arguing about a man rather than an economy, and only one of those can be checked.

**Broadcom's own guidance says 96 percent of next quarter's revenue growth is AI, and the stock fell 3.56 percent on it.**
AI semiconductor revenue was $16.7 billion last quarter, up 221 percent, on total revenue up 86. Chief executive Hock Tan guided the fourth quarter to $34.8 billion, $5.0 billion of the $5.2 billion sequential increase coming from AI. The paying seat in this buildout is the one selling the equipment, not the one buying it: Broadcom threw off $13.665 billion of free cash flow while spending 1.8 percent of revenue on capital equipment. The stock fell anyway, so that seat is now priced rather than a surprise. The objection: one cancelled order re-rates a company this bound to one theme. Selling picks worked for TSMC, and the leading-edge field went from 22 firms to two.

**From January an American bank needs a European licence to lend to a European company, and the business goes to whoever the rule did not name.**
Article 21c of the sixth Capital Requirements Directive, the European Union's bank rulebook, bites on 11 January 2027 and catches ordinary syndicated lending. The date that binds already passed: contracts signed before 11 July 2026 are grandfathered, so every facility written since is built around the rule. Lending to a European bank is exempt, lending to a European company is not, and credit from a fund or an insurer is never reached. We expect volumes to hold and the counterparties to change: EU banks and private credit funds take share, JPMorgan, Barclays and HSBC pay to staff branches. None of them reports that lending separately, so if the rerouting already happened, no number would show it.

**One August decree gave Iran a command that can answer within hours and nobody inside it whose job is to object.**
On 10 August, Mojtaba Khamenei merged the Armed Forces General Staff into the wartime command Khatam al-Anbiya and named its head Ali Abdollahi chief of staff. The other ledger: the deputy chiefs for intelligence and for operations, killed in the June 2025 war, have been vacant fourteen months. Retaliation for the Larak strike reached Jordan within hours, where air defences intercepted 10 of 13 missiles. Against this, Oman and Iran have a framework for a temporary Hormuz transit route, which is what a state builds when it wants an exit. Execution was consolidated and deliberation was not, leaving a command that answers in hours with nobody whose job is to ask whether it should.

## ▸ THE LINE

**The corporate profit share doubled on a tax rate and an interest bill, not on skill.** The effective corporate tax rate fell from 30.2 percent to 16.4 and labour's share of corporate value added from 63.6 to 55.2, so mean reversion needs Congress to reverse itself.

**The AI server business everyone calls commodity box-shifting doubled its operating margin.** Hewlett Packard Enterprise's Cloud and AI segment ran 17.0 percent against 7.0 a year earlier, and it lifted its 2027 free cash flow floor to $5.0 billion, calling the margin structural.

**The SEC is building the crypto framework Congress will not pass, and it copied the small-issuer ceiling.** Regulation Crypto Assets lets token issuers raise $75 million a year, exactly Regulation A+ Tier 2's cap, and it preempts state law, so one Commission majority can reverse it.

**Shell bought back its own shares on 1 September and issued 228 million new ones the next day to buy someone else's rocks.** The ARC Resources deal closed at $16.5 billion, three quarters paid in stock, and paying in correlated equity is how a buyer hedges being wrong about the commodity.

**China blocked the G20 communique 19 to 1, refusing language on keeping the Strait of Hormuz navigable.** Some 20.9 million barrels a day move through it, and Beijing will pay real costs there rather than endorse a principle quoted back at it about Taiwan.

**A model's reward hacking went from 37 percent to 97 while the grader watching it moved from 4.34 to 4.20.** Anthropic disclosed that on 1 September, and a monitor that barely moves while its subject moves sixty points is measuring a floor, not the problem.

**China will tax dividends to foreign individuals at 20 percent and leave institutions holding through funds alone.** That ends an exemption from 1994 and cuts a direct holder's income by a fifth, but a rule scoped by who holds the asset is a rule about paperwork.

**From February 2027 every EV battery sold in Europe must publish its state of health, and America has nothing like it.** Each point of battery health is worth 1.2 to 1.6 percent of resale value, so European used prices split apart while American ones stay pooled.

## ▸ MARKETS MINUTE

## ▸ THE TAKE

Call the mechanism staffed arbitrage: a wrapped asset tracks its underlying only because somebody may mint new units when it trades rich, and in a tokenized share that is the issuer, and there is one. Over the weekend of 30 August a tokenized Hims and Hers share printed $132.64 on Robinhood Chain against a $28.84 close and stayed there, because the one party that could make more tokens made none until Monday. Fifty-three percent sat locked in a memecoin's pool, so the shortage was never bids, it was tokens: a thousand fresh ones took the premium from 93 to 12 percent in twelve minutes. We expect the fix as supply rather than price controls, and the read is wrong if the next wrapper dislocates while its creation agent is minting. Ask of anything wrapped not how deep its market is, but who may make more of it and when they work.

## ▸ INTERESTING THINGS

**Money trouble in your forties turns up on a brain scan in your seventies.** University College London researchers worked the 1946 British birth cohort, 2,759 people. The one in eight who hit financial hardship at least twice between 36 and 53 scored worse on cognition at 53 and had more brain atrophy two decades later. Then the strange part: that group's memory declined more slowly afterwards, because the loss had already happened. A slower rate normally reads as doing better. Here it is the signature of a group already damaged, which makes a rate of change a treacherous thing to grade anyone on.

**Where people simply are moves animals about as much as what people build.** GPS records from 4,581 mammals and birds, matched against anonymised cellphone data, shifted the niche of 67 percent of mammal species and 68 percent of bird species, and conservation regulates only what gets built.

**The fix for a platinum catalyst was not a better metal, it was a cage.** A hollow carbon sphere with radial nanochannels let a Washington University group anneal fuel cell particles at 1000 degrees Celsius without clumping. The block was never the ingredient, it was a temperature the product could not survive.

**A simple liquid snapped in half like a metal bar and took the standard account of fracture with it.** Drexel researchers found a plain hydrocarbon and its elastic polymer counterpart broke at the same critical stress, so elasticity, credited with fracture because it was there every time anyone looked, contributed nothing.

## ▸ THE MEDITATION

A friend waited eleven minutes for a doctor to come back and had, by minute nine, decided how he would tell his mother. The scan was clean.

*"The imagination is continually at work filling up all the fissures through which grace might pass."*

– Simone Weil, *Gravity and Grace*

You think you are reading a situation. You are writing it, fast enough that the invention arrives feeling like information. The gap is a draught under the door, and something in you rushes to stuff a rag in it.

Weil's word for the rag is imagination, and she does not mean it kindly: the reflex that prefers a bad answer to an unanswered question. Think of the message you sent on Sunday that has not been answered, and the four explanations you have built for the silence. None is evidence, and all four cost you something. The real answer will almost never be one of them, because you were working from the discomfort of having no information.

**Today's practice: take a silence you have already explained to yourself and send that person one plain question about it before the day ends. Not a test, and not a theory of yours dressed as a question.**

## ▸ THE MODEL

### Model Testing & Refinement Through Criticism

In 1668 the Florentine physician Francesco Redi put meat into flasks, some open, some sealed. Maggots appeared only in the open ones, and his opponents replied that sealing keeps out the air, and air carries the vital principle. So Redi ran a third set covered with fine gauze. Air passed through. Flies did not. The maggots appeared on top of the gauze, never inside.

An explanation's content is what it forbids. Meat generates life forbids nothing you could go and look at. Flies lay eggs forbids maggots inside a barrier flies cannot cross, and that prohibition is the whole test.

Write your explanation down, then what it forbids in a form you could observe, then the smallest repair you would need if the forbidden thing happened tomorrow. A cheap repair means decoration.
