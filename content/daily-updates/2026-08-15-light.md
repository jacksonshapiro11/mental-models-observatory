### The Sale Moved to June and July Took the Blame

*Retail sales fell for the first time in nine months mostly because one retailer moved its summer sale into June, so July was measured against a July that had it. The shape repeated all day. A rumoured buyer's backer set what a software company was worth. A holder of 82 percent of the votes set a takeout price. A regulator's list of certified yards, not the price of steel, sets the floor under a ship. When the deciding number sits outside the transaction, the adjustment arrives not as an event you can trade but as a level you find you were already living at. The drone tariff is the exception, and the government wrote that schedule itself.*

## ▸ THE UPDATE

**One retailer's decision to move its summer sale into June is the largest single reason American retail sales fell 0.6 percent in July.**
Consensus looked for a gain. Strip motor vehicles and gasoline and the drop is 0.2 percent, inside a confidence interval that includes zero. What broke is nonstore retail, down 2.2 percent, sitting on a calendar: Amazon ran Prime Day in late June this year and mid-July last year. The honest objection is that one month is noise, and the year-over-year series, up 5.0 percent, says no household changed its mind. But the monthly print is what the Fed reads on the day, and seasonal adjustment was never built to absorb one company's sale.

**Riot's mining rigs run out of depreciation in 2029 and the leases meant to replace them have not begun, which makes its bitcoin construction financing rather than treasury.**
Riot Platforms, the largest US-listed bitcoin miner, mined last quarter at a cash cost of $49,912 while the value of each coin it produced fell to $71,667 from $98,800. Coverage read its $90,631 all-in cost as mining underwater; excluding depreciation, which curtailing cannot avoid, it is cash positive. The schedule is the real disclosure: $472.1 million of it remains, running off through 2029, then nothing. Against the same power interconnect Riot has contracted roughly $9.8 billion of twenty-year revenue, none of it earned and the tenant a frontier AI lab it will not name. The asset was never the machines. It was the power line.

**Z.ai published attack scores for its new model and then refused to release the model, and the refusal is the best evidence the scores are real.**
GLM-5.3 launched on 14 August from Z.ai, the Chinese lab behind the GLM series. On the CyberGym benchmark it went from 77.2 percent to 84.5, ahead of GPT-5.6 Sol at 83.6. Then the weights did not ship, though Z.ai had put the previous model's out within days. It says roughly two weeks, after safety hardening. The cheaper explanation is that the lab simply found something to charge for and safety is the flattering cover. But it is already selling the model through a coding plan, so revenue never required holding the weights back. The security researcher Alex Stamos was asked this week for the case against 2027 and 2028 being chaotic cyber warfare and said he did not have much of one.

**Since June 2025 the scrap value that every ship valuation treats as a hard floor has quietly stopped being a price and started being a permit.**
Every valuation carries a floor: below some freight rate the owner sells the hull for steel, and that bid is the bottom of net asset value. It assumes a buyer is legally allowed to exist. On 26 June 2025 the Hong Kong Convention, the maritime industry's ship-recycling treaty, entered force and required an authorised yard. Bangladesh recycled 57 ships in the first half of 2025 against 89 a year earlier. Analysts read thin demolition as a scrap-price signal that reverses when steel firms, a reading with fifty years behind it. It will not reverse here, because the binding input is a certificate. A bulker whose nearest yard is full does not wait. It trades.

**The new American drone tariff hits finished aircraft on 3 September and their parts only in February, and that gap is the policy.** Anyone who starts assembling now gets half a year of cheap foreign parts. A tariff normally protects an industry that exists. This one is trying to summon one.

**Alibaba's newest open-weight model starts charging once your own business passes fifty million dollars of revenue.** Qwen3.8-Max shipped on 12 August under a custom licence replacing Apache 2.0. Open weights were never a philosophy, they were a price, and yours now rises with your success.

## ▸ THE TAKE

A claim's real priority is set by its holder's capacity to impose an immediate, concentrated, attributable cost, not by its contract, which ranks French pensioners above French bondholders. The evidence is a policy variable rather than a spread: France's pension age is 62 against Germany's 67, and Macron needed months of national strikes to legislate 64 by 2030. A junior claimant who cannot be defaulted on is impaired continuously as price rather than at once as an event, so the gradable call is that France legislates no pension age above that path and no benefit cut through 2027. It breaks in Greece, where pensions were cut round after round from 2010 because the Troika held the tap: political seniority survives only while a sovereign keeps market access. Before the debt ratio, read the retirement age against the peer median.

## ▸ INTERESTING THINGS

**Across 170 million years, no dinosaur that was not a bird ever got smaller than a large rabbit.**
Princeton and the American Museum of Natural History put the floor at about 450 grams, two hundred times a bee hummingbird. About 75 percent of living mammal species sit below a line the dinosaurs never crossed, and physiology does not explain it, since the largest of them exceeded 80 tons. The authors think early mammals had already taken the small-body niche and kept it. The famous version runs one way. It ran both ways at once, and flight is what let one lineage escape the floor.

**A twenty-year argument between experiment and theory about melting carbon turned out to be a broken thermometer.** Livermore melted diamond at three times the pressure at Earth's centre and found the old readings off by over a thousand degrees, so fusion capsules can take gentler shocks worth about three times the energy gain.

**A model reading ordinary microscope slides guessed an organ's age better than the chemical clocks built to do it.** Trained on 25,712 slides, it hit a mean error of 4.9 years and its gaps predicted eight diseases including Alzheimer's. That signal has sat in hospital archives for a century.

## ▸ THE MEDITATION

Samuel Johnson chose a harder word than tending.

*"If a man does not make new acquaintance as he advances through life, he will soon find himself left alone. A man, Sir, should keep his friendship in constant repair."*
– Samuel Johnson, recorded by Boswell

Repair is what you do to a thing that decays on its own whether or not you attend to it. The mistake is assuming you would feel it decaying. You do not, because the feeling does not decay. Go two years without speaking to someone and the warmth sits where you left it. That is memory, and memory is free. The relationship is a different object: it runs on contact, and contact is not free. You know the specific person, whose life you follow closely enough to know their news and whom you have not called since a date you would have to look up. Following is not contact. It removes the only reason you ever had to reach out, which was not knowing.

**Today's practice: pick the three people whose lives you know best and have not spoken to, and put their names where you must see them weekly. Following told you everything except that you were drifting, so the prompt must come from outside it.**

## ▸ THE MODEL

### Logic & Optimization Under Constraints

In 1945 George Stigler asked what it would cost to keep a man alive: 77 foods at 1939 prices, nine nutritional requirements, cheapest combination wins. He could not solve it and guessed $39.93. Two years later nine clerks on desk calculators found the true minimum, $39.69. The answer was five foods: wheat flour, cabbage, spinach, dried navy beans, and 2.57 pounds of beef liver a year. Nutritionally perfect, and nothing a person would eat twice. An optimum is a property of the constraint set, not of your objective, and anything you did not write down is free to the solver, spent to zero. Before optimising anything, ask what you left out because it was hard to measure, then ask whether you would live inside the answer for a year.
