## 2. REPETITION WITHOUT ADVANCEMENT — the REPETITION LAW

**Two instruments disagree, and the mechanical one loses.**

**Mechanical** (fingerprint-token overlap with any earlier unit, 30-day lookback, census not sample):

| window | units | flagged at overlap ≥3 |
|---|---|---|
| MAY | 672 | **187 = 27.8%** |
| LAST 30 | 406 | **71 = 17.5%** |

**Judged** (the 101-unit sample): **13.9%.**

🔴 **The mechanical detector fails its own calibration case.** The confirmed repeat —
2026-08-14 `signal:2` re-running 2026-08-01 `signal:2` — scores an overlap of **2**, below the
threshold of 3, sharing only `america` and `ferc`. Meanwhile the top-scoring pairs (overlap 8-10) are
**Hungary's May 9 vote on consecutive days** and **the Iran strikes across a week** — running stories,
which the law explicitly permits when they advance the reader's model.

**Entity overlap cannot tell a continuation from a repetition.** That distinction is the entire
content of the law, and it is a judgment, not a string match. Any future mechanical novelty gate
built on overlap will have this exact failure shape: loud on legitimate continuity, silent on the real
re-run.

**What the judge catches that no string match would:** *"Same model-slug reused: linked 05-18 and
05-23 already"* · *"Same decision-delay self-audit structural move as 2026-04-30's practice"* ·
*"Reuses the Take's Aug-2 queue-as-option move"* · *"Restates markets-macro:2's AMD-broadening claim,
same 33%/$9.89B figures reused"* — **that last one is a repeat inside the same night's brief.**

## 4. TOP-10 SKIPS THAT BECAME UNDENIABLE — and why this number should not be quoted

Operationalised as: a `####` candidate block in day D's `daily-intelligence` file whose entities do not
appear in day D's brief, but do appear in D+1's or D+2's.

| window | candidates read | not picked that day | returned within 2 days |
|---|---|---|---|
| MAY | 669 | 277 = 41% | **138 = 49.8% of skips** |
| LAST 30 | 517 | 220 = 43% | **122 = 55.5% of skips** |

🔴 **I do not believe this measure and I am reporting it so it can be discarded on the record.** The
top-10 lists it produces are contaminated with things that are not stories: *"AI / TECH"*,
*"Crypto"*, *"Equities PM Digest (New captures)"*, and article titles like *"Zvi Mowshowitz —
Childhood And Education #17."*

**The reason is structural, not a bug I can fix.** `daily-intelligence` records **sources read**, not
**stories considered and rejected.** One source informs several units; a source that goes unused is not
a skipped story; and a story that was weighed and consciously declined leaves **no trace anywhere in
the repository.** A skip rate computed from sources is measuring reading, not selecting.

**The missed-boat class cannot be measured from existing artifacts. That is the finding.** It is
exactly what Phase B's candidate funnel exists to create — 3-5 angles per deep slot with the losers
logged — and this audit is the evidence for why that funnel is the unlock rather than a nicety.
**Until it exists, every selection number this system produces is conditional on what got written
down.**
