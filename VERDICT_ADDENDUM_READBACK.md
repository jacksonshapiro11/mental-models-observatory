## 2. The audio measurement — measurement accepted, inference resisted, three ways

The numbers are real and the caveats are honestly stated. The dual read-back is adopted into
Stage 0, gated on your Part 4 fix. But the framing — "the only stage doing the thing this project
is trying to do," "may reorder everything" — runs ahead of the evidence, and on the exact axis this
project spent today demoting:

**(a) Shorter sentences are the proxy, not the thing.** A lossy shortener posts identical columns.
The GPT pass has no claims file and no intent; it can smooth the vague-wrong into *fluent*
vague-wrong, and the Burger King inversion — a missing anchor, not a long sentence — likely
survives it untouched. The dual read-back decides, and the deciding columns are grades, not means.

**(b) The owner was asked whether the collaborator listened or read. His answer: not reliably
known.** So the hypothesis gets no free evidence, and the dual read-back carries the full
evidentiary weight on its own. His stated prior, which this reviewer shares: **an argument that
does not make sense is too complex for both surfaces.** The deep failure class — unfinished
thoughts, missing anchors, the Warsh muddle — survives any smoothing pass, because the smoother
does not know the intent. Only sentence tangle differs by surface. The design therefore stands as
built: fix the source, both surfaces inherit, the monitor guards the seam. Your hypothesis lives or
dies on the test's grades, not on its sentence means.

**(c) The upstream option, as stated, reintroduces the mechanism that wrote the Warsh error.** A
rewrite pass moved into drafting is an unverified correction — the exact class this system already
burned on, twice. If the dual read-back shows a large audio advantage, the conclusion is not
"replace the loop with the stylist." It is "give the stylist the claims file and keep the Reader
behind it" — a claim-anchored rewrite pass whose output re-enters the read-back. That is the loop
with a different redraft author, and it may indeed be the cheaper variant. Price it then, with
grades in hand, not now, with sentence means.
