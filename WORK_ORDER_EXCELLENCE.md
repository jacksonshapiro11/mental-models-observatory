# PART 3 — THE COCKPIT SELF-READ ✅ LIVE (doctrine)

After each unit: **one fresh blind reader, artifact text in-prompt, no tools, no files.** Claim and
why-care back. Mismatch → **one redraft, then proceed.** Logged to the claims sidecar as
`"self_read": "PASS" | "REDRAFTED-ONCE"`.

**This is the cheap fix point, not a new authority.** A unit repaired here costs one redraft; the same
unit repaired at the gate costs a rewrite inside a deadline. ~15-20 spawns a night, and the point is
that they are cheap.

# HOW THIS FAILS

1. **The canon becomes an archive.** Guard: hard cap 30, every addition names its pruned entry.
2. **The cockpit becomes a ritual.** Guard: the redraft rate is printed weekly and a zero is a finding.
3. **Three checks, one blind spot.** The cockpit reader, the gate readers and the funnel all read what
   was WRITTEN. **None sees the story that was never drafted** — the funnel's losers are the closest
   thing, and they are only the losers someone bothered to log.
4. **Cost.** ~15-20 cockpit spawns plus 3 readers × 24 full-brief units. Guard: the hard fallback is
   unchanged — **any error, any timeout, skip and log. THE BRIEF ALWAYS SHIPS.**
5. **The last construction order is not the last one.** Guard: this document says so on its face, and
   day-30 is an evaluation with a fixed agenda.
